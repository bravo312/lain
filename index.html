Lain

A private, self-hosted personal assistant.

Lain runs on its owner's own computer at home and is used by one person only: its owner. It helps with day-to-day logistics, such as reading the owner's calendar, summarizing incoming email, drafting replies and proposing calendar events.

Lain is not a public product or service. It has no other users, and no one else can sign in to it.

Any action that changes something in the owner's Google account, such as sending email or editing the calendar, happens only after the owner explicitly confirms it.
