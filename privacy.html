Privacy policy

Lain, a private personal assistant. Effective October 19, 2026.

Who uses Lain

Lain is used by a single person, its owner, who is also its operator. It is not offered to the public, and it does not collect data from anyone else.

What data Lain accesses

With the owner's permission, granted through Google's sign-in, Lain accesses the owner's own Google account data: Gmail, Google Calendar, Google Drive, Docs, Sheets and Contacts. It uses this data only to assist the owner, for example to summarize email, check the schedule, or draft replies.

Where data is kept

Lain runs on hardware the owner controls at home. Data retrieved from Google is processed there. Brief excerpts may be sent to the AI model provider the owner uses, solely to generate Lain's responses to the owner. Google user data is not sold, not shared with anyone else, not used for advertising, and not used to train AI models.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Lain's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Retention and deletion

Email summaries are kept for up to 30 days. The owner can revoke Lain's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, after which Lain can no longer access the account.
